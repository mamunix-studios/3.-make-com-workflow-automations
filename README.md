## 🛠️ Included Automation Blueprints

### 1. Real-Time Google Sheets Row Monitor & Email Alert
* **File:** `1_google_sheets_new_row_email_alert.json`
* **Trigger:** `Google Sheets — Watch New Rows`
* **Action:** `Gmail — Send an Email`
* **Use Case:** Automatically detects every newly added lead or form entry in a master Google Sheet and dispatches an instant notification email with dynamic row data mapped directly into the message body.

### 2. Automated Client Follow-Up & Onboarding Pipeline
* **File:** `2_google_sheets_to_gmail_client_followup.json`
* **Trigger:** `Google Sheets — Watch New Rows`
* **Action:** `Gmail — Send an Email`
* **Use Case:** Streamlines client onboarding and B2B lead nurturing by sending structured, customized follow-up emails immediately after prospect details are logged into the tracking spreadsheet.

## 🚀 How to Import & Use These Blueprints

Clients and developers can deploy these workflows into any Make.com account in under 2 minutes:

1. Download the desired `.json` blueprint file from this repository.
2. Log in to your **Make.com** dashboard and click **`+ Create a new scenario`**.
3. Click the **`...` (More)** menu icon on the bottom control bar and select **`Import Blueprint`**.
4. Upload the downloaded `.json` file.
5. Click on the **Google Sheets** and **Gmail** modules to connect your own Google account credentials, select your target spreadsheet, and toggle the scenario **ON**.
